BUDGETHELOCK STUDIO

PlatForm - Very Hard

PlatForm can get seriously difficult. You have to time your jumps, make it through gaps, and reach the end without dying. If you die, you start over from the beginning. There's basically no limit to how difficult a level can get.

Ball Horror Remastered - Very Hard

Ball Horror has 10 levels with 10 different monsters, along with buttons, codes, levers, running sections, and more. You have to get through the entire game without dying, so one mistake can ruin the whole run.

HallWay - Medium

HallWay is all about reacting quickly and knowing what to do. You have to listen for the audio cue that tells you an entity is coming, find the closest locker, figure out the fastest way there, and keep track of how long you can stay hidden.

PIXEL SHOOTER X - Medium

You have to fight off monsters while keeping your 3 hearts intact. Good aim and movement are important, and taking too much damage can end your run.

StickRun - Easy

StickRun is pretty simple. Jump over the spikes and try to keep going as the game gets faster. The longer you survive, the harder it gets.
